Our story

A year ago, I was told to go off maida for health reasons. Giving up my favourite treats was hard, but finding good replacements was even harder. The gluten-free desserts in the market didn’t taste good, and I was never sure what went into them or how fresh they really were.

So I started baking my own, with jowar, ragi, besan, oats, buckwheat and almond flour, sweetened with jaggery and khand, and made with real chocolate (couverture). What began in my own kitchen for my own health became The Kind Oven: treats I’m happy to eat myself and proud to share with your family.
